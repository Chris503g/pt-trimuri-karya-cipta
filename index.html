About us

A local Indonesian company with two complementary strengths.

Our company profile positions TKC around general trading and engineering system integration, supporting customers in Indonesia.

PT Trimuri Karya Cipta was established on 18 October 2006. Our General Trading business supports procurement of mechanical, electrical, IT and stationery products.

Alongside trading, our engineering team works as a system integrator for automation systems, with experience in mechatronic design & build and Special Purpose Machines (SPM).
