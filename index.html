Company / since 18 October 2006

General trading and engineering since 2006.

PT Trimuri Karya Cipta is a local Indonesian company supporting customers with procurement of mechanical, electrical, IT and stationery products. Alongside general trading, our engineering team builds automation systems and handles mechatronic design and build for Special Purpose Machines.
